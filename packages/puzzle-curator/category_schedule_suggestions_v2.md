# IPL 2026 — Category Suggestions v2 (Historic Squads Edition)

Key change from v1: Green slot now uses **historic season squads** (2008–2024) instead of 2025.
These are much better trivia — players remember iconic rosters, not last year's squad.

Difficulty mapping:
- 🟨 Yellow = Easy (current 2026 squads, match-day relevant)
- 🟩 Green = Medium (historic squads from memorable seasons)
- 🟦 Blue = Hard (career stats, records, coaches)
- 🟪 Purple = Hardest (niche stats, obscure categories)

Historic season selection rationale:
- 2008 — inaugural season, iconic rosters
- 2009 — second season, many remember it
- 2010/2011 — golden era
- 2012/2013 — KKR dynasty, CSK dominance
- 2013 — CSK/MI/RR golden era
- 2016 — RCB's famous near-miss season (Kohli 973 runs)
- 2022 — GT/LSG debut season
- 2023/2024 — recent but not current
